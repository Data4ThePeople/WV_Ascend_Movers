# Hero prompts: west-virginia-ascend

Final size 1680x1080 (landscape, about 14:9). Generate at 16:9 or 3:2; `hero fit`
crops from the center. No text, logos or real people's likenesses in the image.

## 1. One worker on the ridge (recommended)

Wide landscape photograph-style illustration at early morning. A single person
sits on a rock outcrop on a forested Appalachian ridge in West Virginia, laptop
open, coffee mug beside them, seen from behind and slightly to the side. Layers
of blue-green mountains and river fog fill the valley below, with a small town
and a few houses visible far down. Calm, warm light from the left. Muted
natural colors with deep blue shadows. Keep the person small in the frame, in
the left third, with open sky in the upper half for space. No text.

## 2. Whale and worker

Wide, painterly editorial illustration. On the left, an enormous whale made of
steel girders and factory parts floats in a hazy sky above a flat construction
site with cranes, unfinished. On the right, across a river, a single small
figure walks up a green West Virginia hillside carrying a laptop bag toward a
modest house with a lit window. Dusk light, dark blue-gray sky, warm light only
on the house and the figure. Muted palette. No text.

## 3. Moving day, one house

Wide photograph-style scene. A small house on a hillside street in a West
Virginia mountain town, autumn trees, a family unloading a few boxes from a
car while one adult carries a laptop bag inside. Mountains behind, soft late
afternoon light. Faces not visible or turned away. Natural, quiet, documentary
feel. No text, no readable signs or plates.
