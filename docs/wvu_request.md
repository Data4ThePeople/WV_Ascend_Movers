# Data request to WVU (draft for Eric to send)

**To:** WVU Brad and Alys Smith Outdoor Economic Development Collaborative
(Danny Twilley, or the program's general contact on ascendwv.com / oedc.wvu.edu)

**Subject:** Data request on Ascend WV for a Data 4 The People piece

---

Hello,

I run Data 4 The People, a nonprofit data journalism project. I am writing a
piece on Ascend WV as an example of economic development that pays workers
directly, and I would like to get the program's numbers right.

The figures I have found in news coverage differ from each other. For
example, I have seen participant counts of 509, 704, and "more than 700," and
retention rates from 96% to 98%. Could you share the following, or point me to
where they are published?

1. Participants (the remote workers themselves) and total new residents,
   including family members, by year of arrival and by community.
2. How the program defines retention, and the retention rate by cohort.
3. Total program spending by year, including incentives paid and operating
   costs, and the share from private gifts, WVU, and state sources.
4. The 2023 economic impact study, and the method behind the $500 million and
   $580 million figures.
5. Anything the program has learned about how many participants might have
   moved to West Virginia without the incentive, such as a survey question or
   a comparison with applicants who were not selected.

I plan to write the piece the week of November 16, 2026. Anything you can share
before then would help.

Thank you,
Eric Pachman
Data 4 The People
